Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026Applies to: Atlas for Discord
In short: Atlas only reads messages sent to it, keeps the last 12 per channel in temporary memory, and never stores, sells, or shares them. Its AI runs on our own hardware, not a third-party AI company.

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Atlas ("Atlas", "the Bot", or "the Service"), a Discord bot created and operated by Ruka ("we", "us", or "our"), processes, why, and for how long. Atlas is designed to process as little information as possible and to keep it only briefly. This policy should be read together with our Terms of Service.

Information we process

Atlas only processes messages that are directed to it. It does not read or store other messages in a server.

InformationWhen it is processedPurpose
Message contentWhen you mention Atlas, reply to one of its messages, or send it a direct messageTo generate a response
Display nameWith each message aboveSo Atlas can tell participants apart in a conversation
Server, channel, message and user IDsWith each message aboveTo deliver the response in the correct place

Atlas does not collect email addresses, passwords, payment details, location, or device information, and it does not use cookies or analytics.

How information is used

Information is used only to generate and deliver Atlas's responses and to keep a short conversation history so replies make sense in context. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training artificial intelligence models.

Where processing happens

Atlas's artificial intelligence model runs on hardware operated by us. Your messages are processed there and are not sent to any third-party AI provider. Messages are transmitted through Discord, which processes them under its own Privacy Policy.

Retention

Atlas keeps the most recent 12 messages per channel in temporary memory only. This history is never written to disk or stored in a database. It is deleted:

  • immediately, when anyone in the channel types @Atlas reset;
  • automatically, as newer messages replace older ones; and
  • entirely, whenever the Bot restarts.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Information may be disclosed only if required by law.

Your choices and rights

You can clear Atlas's memory of a channel at any time with @Atlas reset, and you can stop Atlas from processing your messages by not mentioning it, not replying to it, and not messaging it directly. Server administrators can remove Atlas at any time. Because Atlas does not keep lasting records, there is generally no stored data to access or export. You may still contact us with any privacy request.

Children

Atlas is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required to use Discord in their country (13 in most countries). We do not knowingly process information from children below that age.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information while it is processed, including keeping conversation history in memory only and never storing it permanently. No system is completely secure, so please do not share passwords, tokens, or other sensitive personal information with Atlas.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" at the top of this page. Your continued use of Atlas after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
